# Content purpose review — October 28, 2026

## Scope and limits

Reviewed the route inventory and rendered heading structure for 14 calculators, 16 blog posts and four guides. Compared the overlapping concrete and brick resources in detail against current source. This is an editorial and functional review, not a determination of Google's AdSense classification. The rejection message names low value content without identifying specific URLs.

Repeated formulas or a shared article layout are not, by themselves, evidence of a policy violation. The changes below address reader purpose and a material product mismatch. No pages were removed, redirected or newly noindexed. Existing category/tag and personal utility noindex settings remain.
