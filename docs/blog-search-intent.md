## Implementation

Unique titles and descriptions feed metadata and social previews. Each article opens with a direct answer with assumptions, adds a comparison section and question, and links to related articles and its calculator. Existing source citations, image alt text, table of contents and Article/Breadcrumb structured data remain. FAQ content is for readers; no rich-result promise is made.

## Measurement after deployment

Verify rendered title, canonical, indexability, internal links and sitemap URLs. In Google Search Console, inspect representative URLs and review impressions, clicks, CTR and queries by page over comparable periods. Use observed queries to improve helpful coverage; do not create duplicate pages for near-identical keywords. Search Console data and live deployment have not been verified for this change.
